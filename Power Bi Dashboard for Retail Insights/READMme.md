📊 **Retail Dataset Analysis Dashboard Project**

🎯 **Project Objectives**

The main objective of this project is to analyze retail sales performance across multiple dimensions and provide actionable insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard. Specific goals include:

Understanding overall sales performance (total sales, total sale price, customer count, countries served).

Identifying top-performing products by sales and quantity.

Tracking monthly sales trends to spot seasonality and growth patterns.

Analyzing customer-level sales and product purchase behavior.

Visualizing sales distribution across different countries.

Assisting retail decision-makers in optimizing sales strategies, inventory planning, and customer targeting.

🛠️ **Tech Stack**

Power BI → For data modeling, visualization, and dashboard creation.

Data Source → Retail dataset (likely from Excel/CSV or SQL database).

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) → Used for calculated measures such as Total Sales, Total Sale Price, Monthly Aggregations.

Power Query (M Language) → For data cleaning, transformation, and shaping before modeling.

Mapping Tools (Microsoft Maps, TomTom, OpenStreetMap) → For geographical sales visualization.

Identifies top-selling products such as Paper Craft, Regency Cake Stand, White Hanging Decorations, and Postage.

**Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)**

Indicates strong seasonal impact on sales.

**Quantity by Product Description (Donut Chart)**

Highlights items sold in bulk, like Paper Craft and Medium Ceramic Products.

Supports inventory forecasting.

**Quantity by Customer (Bar Chart)**

Shows which customers buy the most (up to 81K units by top customers).

Useful for customer segmentation and loyalty programs.

Customer Purchase Table

Displays CustomerID and purchased products, useful for personalization and CRM insights.

**Sales by Country (Map Visualization)**

Europe and North America are strong contributors.

Expands understanding of global customer distribution.

✅ **Outcomes / Insights**

Sales are spread across 37 countries, but concentrated in Europe.

A few customers contribute disproportionately high sales, indicating potential for VIP/Loyalty strategies.

Seasonal analysis shows November–December spike, confirming retail’s dependency on holiday sales.

Top product categories dominate revenue, guiding product promotion strategies.

Provides a holistic view of sales performance for decision-making in pricing, marketing, and inventory.
